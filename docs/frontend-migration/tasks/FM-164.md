# FM-164: Mount Stats Charts Only Once They Reach The Viewport

Status: planned Owner:
Feature IDs: F-STATS-MAIN
Component IDs: None
API IDs: None
Depends on: None
Blocks: FM-169

## Outcome

Opening the stats dashboard stops paying for charts nobody has scrolled to. `StatsDashboardPage.tsx:462-474` renders all
five sections in one commit, and those sections construct six `ChartCard`s (`ActivitySection.tsx:45,84`;
`IndexersSection.tsx:282,302`; `SourcesSection.tsx:120`; `DownloadAgeSection.tsx:60`), each mounting its chart
immediately — `ChartCard.tsx:63` is `{showTable ? table : chart}` with no visibility gate — and a `HorizontalBarChart`
can be 900px tall (`HorizontalBarChart.tsx:48`), so most of that SVG work happens far below the fold. Gating the mount
in `ChartCard` fixes every caller at once and is the only place that knows both arms of the chart-XOR-table toggle,
which is why this is one packet rather than five section edits.

## Decision Dependencies

- ADR-0021 (stats redesign; charts secondary, tables the accessibility layer) — binding: deferral must never make a
  statistic unreachable.

## Acceptance

- `ChartCard` mounts its `chart` only once the card intersects the viewport (plus a rootMargin band so it is ready
  slightly before it is reached); until then it renders a placeholder of the chart's own height, so scrollbar length
  and card positions are identical to today at first paint and nothing shifts as charts arrive.
- The placeholder height comes from the chart's real sizing rule, not a guess: the formula inline at
  `HorizontalBarChart.tsx:48` becomes an exported helper used by both the chart and its caller, and each `ChartCard`
  caller passes the resulting height (a fixed-height chart passes its fixed value).
- Once mounted a chart is never unmounted by scrolling. The chart-XOR-table toggle keeps its exact semantics: switching
  to the table unmounts the chart arm, switching back re-mounts immediately with no visibility gate. A card showing its
  table arm does not mount its chart when it scrolls into view.
- ADR-0021 holds: every statistic stays reachable through the table arm, and the toggle button and its `aria-expanded`
  are unchanged.
- Where `IntersectionObserver` is absent (jsdom, older embedded browsers) charts mount eagerly — the feature degrades to
  today's behavior, not to a blank dashboard. `ChartCard.test.tsx` pins both branches with a stubbed observer.
- `StatsDashboardPage.test.tsx` and `IndexersSection.test.tsx` stay green with assertions intact; where a test needs a
  chart present it drives the stub rather than deleting the assertion.
- `stats.spec.ts`'s two chart assertions keep their force against the real backend: each chart is scrolled to before
  being asserted visible, and the deselected-family `toHaveCount(0)` is checked with that card in view. The placeholder
  must **not** carry the chart's `data-testid`.
- `F-STATS-MAIN` records the placeholder selector and one gap line stating that below-fold charts mount on scroll,
  citing the eager fallback.
- Screenshot strip per `../README.md` *Visual Gate*: the dashboard at first paint at 1280x800 (below-fold placeholders
  at full height), the same page scrolled to the bottom, and 390x844 at first paint.
